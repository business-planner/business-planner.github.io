FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about business planning with Business Planner.

What is a business planner web application? ▼
A business planner web application is a digital tool that helps entrepreneurs, startups, and business owners create comprehensive business plans, model financials, track goals, and develop strategic roadmaps. Business Planner provides all of these capabilities in a single, easy-to-use platform accessible from any browser.
Do I need any business planning experience to use Business Planner? ▼
Not at all. Business Planner is designed for entrepreneurs at every stage. Our guided prompts, pre-built templates, and step-by-step process walk you through every aspect of building a professional business plan — even if you've never written one before.
Can I create financial projections without an accounting background? ▼
Yes. Our financial modeling tools are built for non-accountants. Simply enter your assumptions — revenue targets, cost estimates, and growth rates — and Business Planner automatically generates income statements, cash flow forecasts, and break-even analyses with visual charts.
Is my business plan data saved securely? ▼
Your data is saved locally in your browser's local storage, giving you full ownership and control. No account is required to start planning. For cloud backup and team collaboration features, you can optionally create a free account.
What industries and business types does Business Planner support? ▼
Business Planner supports all industries and business models including SaaS, e-commerce, retail, restaurants, professional services, manufacturing, non-profits, and more. With over 120 industry-specific templates, you'll find a starting point tailored to your specific business context.
Can I use Business Planner to prepare for investor meetings? ▼
Absolutely. Business Planner is designed with investor-readiness in mind. The structured business plan framework follows the format that investors and lenders expect. You can also use our pitch deck generator to transform your plan into a professional presentation tailored for funding conversations.
How does the SWOT analysis tool work? ▼
The SWOT analysis tool presents a visual four-quadrant matrix where you can add, edit, and organize your business's Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats. Items are easily managed with inline editing. The completed analysis can be exported as part of your business plan or as a standalone document.
