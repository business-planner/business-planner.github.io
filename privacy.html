Privacy Policy

At Business Planner, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This policy outlines how we handle your information when you use our web application.

1. Data Collection

We collect minimal information to provide our services. This includes:

  • Local Storage: Your business plans, SWOT analyses, and financial data are stored locally in your browser. We do not transmit this data to our servers unless you explicitly create a cloud account.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics (gtag.js) to understand how users interact with our site to improve the planning experience.

2. How We Use Data

Information collected is used to maintain the application’s functionality, provide customer support, and analyze aggregate trends. We never sell your personal or business data to third parties.

3. Cookies

We use cookies to remember your preferences and for analytical purposes. You can choose to disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of the planner may not function correctly.

4. Your Rights

Since much of your data is stored locally, you have the right to delete your data at any time by clearing your browser cache or using the "Reset" functions within the app.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at privacy@business-planner.example.com.
